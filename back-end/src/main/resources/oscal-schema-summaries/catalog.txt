OSCAL Catalog — Metaschema short-name "oscal-catalog", namespace http://csrc.nist.gov/ns/oscal/1.0

Top-level: catalog
Required children of catalog:
  - uuid (flag, UUID)
  - metadata (assembly)
Optional:
  - params (list of parameter assemblies)
  - controls (list of control assemblies)
  - groups (list of group assemblies, may be nested)
  - back-matter

Key Metapath addresses (relative to catalog/):
  - metadata/title (required, non-empty string)
  - metadata/last-modified (required, dateTime)
  - metadata/version (required, string)
  - metadata/oscal-version (required, string)
  - metadata/parties (list)
  - metadata/parties/party@uuid (UUID, unique within metadata)
  - metadata/roles/role@id (string, unique within metadata)
  - controls/control@id (string, unique within catalog)
  - controls/control/title (required)
  - controls/control/params/param@id
  - controls/control/parts/part@name (string, e.g. "statement")
  - controls/control/parts/part/@id
  - controls/control/links/link@href
  - groups/group@id
  - groups/group/controls/control@id
  - groups/group/groups (recursive)
  - back-matter/resources/resource@uuid

Common constraint patterns:
  <expect target="controls/control" test="title and string-length(title)>0">
    <message>[custom: r1] every control must have a non-empty title</message>
  </expect>
  <has-cardinality target="controls/control" min-occurs="1"/>
  <is-unique target="controls/control" id="catalog-controls-unique">
    <key-field target="@id"/>
  </is-unique>
  <allowed-values target="controls/control/parts/part/@name" allow-other="yes">
    <enum value="statement"/><enum value="guidance"/><enum value="example"/>
  </allowed-values>
  <matches target="metadata/version" regex="^[0-9]+\\.[0-9]+(\\.[0-9]+)?$"/>

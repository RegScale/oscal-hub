# AI Analytics

The AI Analytics page at `/org-admin/ai-analytics` gives you visibility into how — and how much — your organization is using AI features. Use it to track spending trends, understand which tools drive the most AI activity, and identify heavy users.

---

## What it shows

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Operations over time** | Line or bar chart of AI requests per day/week/month |
| **Cost estimate** | Cumulative estimated spend in USD for the selected period |
| **Model breakdown** | Distribution of requests across Claude variants (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus) |
| **Top use cases** | Which features generate the most AI activity (e.g., Catalog Wizard, Component Wizard) |
| **Per-user breakdown** | Operations and cost attributed to each member |

---

## Review AI usage

<Steps>
<Step title="Open AI Analytics">
Navigate to `/org-admin/ai-analytics`.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a time range">
Use the date-range picker at the top of the page to select the period you want to analyze (e.g., last 7 days, last 30 days, or a custom range).
</Step>
<Step title="Review the overview charts">
The operations-over-time chart shows request volume. The cost estimate panel shows the running total for the period. Hover over chart data points for exact values.
</Step>
<Step title="Examine model distribution">
The model breakdown panel shows which Claude variants were used and what proportion of requests — and cost — each represents.
</Step>
<Step title="Drill into use cases and users">
Scroll to the **Top Use Cases** and **Per-User Breakdown** tables. Click a use case or user row (if drilldown is available) to filter the charts to that segment.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tips and limits

- **Cost estimates are approximations.** The displayed costs are calculated from Anthropic's published per-token pricing at the time the request was made. Actual charges come from Anthropic directly; small discrepancies between the estimate and your Anthropic invoice are normal.
- **Data is near-real-time.** There may be a short lag (typically minutes) between an AI operation and its appearance in the analytics dashboard.
- **No retroactive data.** AI usage is tracked only from the date AI was first enabled for your org. Historical activity before enabling cannot be reconstructed.
- **Use analytics to right-size your model.** If the per-user or use-case cost is higher than expected, consider switching to a lighter model on the [AI Settings](/guide/org-admin/ai-settings) page.

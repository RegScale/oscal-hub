# Profile

Your Profile page is the central place to manage your personal information, upload a profile logo, and create service account tokens for programmatic API access. You can reach it at any time by clicking your username or avatar in the top-right corner of the navigation bar and selecting **Profile**.

---

## What you can edit

The profile form lets you update the following fields:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **First name** | Shown in the user menu and header |
| **Last name** | Combined with first name in display contexts |
| **Email** | Used for notifications and password recovery |
| **Phone number** | Optional; used for org-level contact info |
| **Job title** | Optional; visible to org administrators |
| **Organization affiliation** | Optional; your employer or affiliated entity |
| **Street, City, State, ZIP** | Optional address fields |

Email changes take effect immediately. If your organization uses email for MFA recovery, update this field carefully.

---

## How to update your profile

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the user menu">
Click your **username or avatar** in the top-right corner of any page. A dropdown menu appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Profile">
Select **Profile** from the dropdown. You are taken to `/profile`.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit your information">
Update any fields you want to change. All fields except email are optional — clear a field to remove a previously saved value.
</Step>
<Step title="Click Save Changes">
Scroll to the bottom of the form and click **Save Changes**. A success message confirms the update.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
If you need to change your password, use the dedicated **Change Password** form at `/change-password`. Changing your password from the Profile page is no longer supported — the profile form handles personal info only.
</Callout>

## Profile logo

A profile logo (avatar) is displayed in the navigation bar user menu and in certain org-facing views where your identity is shown alongside documents you own or edited.

**Supported formats:** JPG and PNG. Recommended size: at least 128 × 128 pixels.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click your current avatar">
On the Profile page, click the circular avatar or placeholder image at the top of the form.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a file">
Your system file picker opens. Select a JPG or PNG image from your computer.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview and save">
A preview of the new image appears. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the form to upload and apply the logo.
</Step>
</Steps>

The new logo appears immediately in the top navigation bar after saving.

## Service account tokens

Service account tokens let scripts, CI/CD pipelines, and external tools authenticate to the OSCAL Hub API without using your personal login credentials. They are managed from the **Service Account Tokens** section at the bottom of the Profile page.

For full instructions on creating and using service account tokens — including API usage examples and security best practices — see [Service Account Tokens](/guide/account/service-tokens).

---

## Tips and limits

- **Email uniqueness:** Each email address can only be associated with one OSCAL Hub account. If you receive an error when updating your email, it is likely already in use by another account.
- **Logo file size:** Keep logo files under 2 MB to ensure fast uploads.
- **Display name:** OSCAL Hub displays your first and last name together wherever your identity appears. Fill in both fields for the best experience.
- **Organization affiliation** on your profile is distinct from the org you belong to in OSCAL Hub. The profile field is free-text metadata; actual org membership is managed through invitations and access requests.

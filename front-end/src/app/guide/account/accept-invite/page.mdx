# Accept an Invite

When an organization administrator invites you to their OSCAL Hub workspace, you receive an email containing a unique invitation link. Clicking that link takes you to the Accept Invitation page, where you can join the organization — either by signing in with an existing account or by creating a new one on the spot.

---

## What to expect from the invitation email

The invitation email includes:

- The **name of the organization** you are being invited to join.
- The **role** you will be assigned (for example, Member, Analyst, or Org Admin).
- A **unique invitation link** in the format `/accept-invite?token=<token>`.

The link is valid for **7 days** by default. After it expires, the page will display an error and you will need to contact the person who sent the invite to request a new one.

---

## Accepting the invite

<Steps>
<Step title="Click the invitation link">
Open the invitation email and click the **Accept Invitation** button or link. Your browser opens OSCAL Hub at `/accept-invite?token=...`.
</Step>
<Step title="Review the invitation details">
The page displays the organization name, the inviting admin's name, and the role you are being assigned. Confirm these details are correct.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in or create an account">
- **If you already have an OSCAL Hub account:** Click **Sign in** and enter your credentials. Your existing account will be linked to the organization.
- **If you do not have an account yet:** Enter a new **username** and **password** in the registration fields on the same page. Your new account will be created and immediately associated with the organization.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm and join">
Click the **Accept** or **Join Organization** button. OSCAL Hub processes the invite token and adds your account to the organization.
</Step>
<Step title="Start working">
You are redirected to the OSCAL Hub home page inside the organization's context. All organization-level features — shared Library, org-scoped Authorizations, admin tools (if your role allows) — are now available to you.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## What happens after joining

- You are added to the organization with the **role specified in the invitation**.
- If your account previously had no organization, you are now placed directly in the new org's context.
- If your account **already belonged to other organizations**, you can switch between them using the **org switcher** in the top navigation bar. Switching orgs does not require signing out.
- The invitation link is invalidated immediately after use — it cannot be used to add a second account.

---

## Tips and limits

- **Expired invitations (7 days):** If the link has expired, the page displays an error. Contact your organization admin and ask them to resend the invitation from their admin dashboard.
- **Wrong email address:** If you receive an invite at an email address that is different from the one on your existing OSCAL Hub account, you can still sign in with your existing account — the invite is accepted by clicking the link and then signing in, regardless of which email received the message.
- **Token reuse:** Each invitation token can be accepted only once. If you accidentally close the page before completing the flow, click the link in the original email again — it remains valid until used or expired.
- **Role changes after joining:** If you need a different role than the one specified in the invitation, contact your Org Admin after joining. Roles can be changed from the admin dashboard.

<Callout type="info">
If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO), the invite flow may redirect you to your identity provider's login page rather than the built-in username/password form. Complete the SSO sign-in and you will be returned to OSCAL Hub to finish accepting the invite.
</Callout>
